# Before You Lock The Corpus

Print or copy this checklist before a real project moves from search results to
a locked screening snapshot.

Locking the corpus is a research decision. After the lock, title and abstract
screening, full-text retrieval, and final eligibility decisions all depend on
that snapshot. If the team discovers a search or protocol problem after the
lock, the project may need an amended protocol, a rerun search, and a new
snapshot.

## Lock Meeting

| Item | Done |
| --- | --- |
| Review lead is present or has approved the lock. |  |
| Search reviewer, librarian, or method lead has reviewed the search strategy. |  |
| Reviewer team understands that screening will use the locked snapshot. |  |
| Someone is assigned to record the final lock reason. |  |

## Protocol Readiness

| Question | Pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| Is the research question stable? | The team can state the question without changing population, intervention, concept, comparator, or outcome scope. |
| Are inclusion criteria specific enough? | A reviewer can apply them without asking for hidden assumptions. |
| Are exclusion criteria specific enough? | Wrong population, wrong setting, wrong intervention, wrong design, no usable outcomes, and wrong publication type are handled explicitly. |
| Is the date range intentional? | The team can explain why the start and end dates are appropriate. |
| Is the language policy intentional? | Included, excluded, and translation-follow-up policies are documented. |
| Is the AI policy recorded? | The project says whether AI is not used, assistive only, or excluded from final decisions. |
| Is the full-text policy recorded? | The team knows whether full text is optional, required for inclusion, or manual-only. |

## Search Readiness

| Check | Pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| Providers are justified. | Selected providers match the discipline and claim. Missing databases are named. |
| Query strings are retained. | Exact query text, provider list, date limits, and result limits are visible. |
| Anchor records were checked. | Known relevant papers or reviews appear, or the team records why they do not. |
| Provider failures are reviewed. | Failed search items are rerun, accepted with reason, or handled before lock. |
| Search noise is understood. | Irrelevant records are acceptable because sensitivity is still adequate. |
| Duplicates are reviewed. | Duplicate clusters are inspected and representative records are acceptable. |

## Corpus Readiness

| Check | Pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| Corpus count is plausible. | The team understands the number of unique records entering the lock. |
| Query links are plausible. | Provider and query provenance explain how records entered the corpus. |
| Metadata issues are reviewed. | Missing abstracts, missing identifiers, retractions, and strange venues are considered. |
| Out-of-scope records are expected. | Noise is not a surprise; it will be handled during screening. |
| No obvious search error remains. | The team would not immediately change the search after seeing the corpus. |

## Stop Conditions

Do not lock the corpus if any of these are true:

- the protocol is still changing;
- the team cannot explain the provider choices;
- known relevant records are missing and no reason is recorded;
- a provider failed and the team has not decided what to do;
- inclusion and exclusion criteria are too vague for independent reviewers;
- the corpus appears dominated by irrelevant records because the search is
  poorly formed;
- the team wants to make a claim that the search cannot support.

## Lock Reason Template

Use a clear audit reason:

```text
Corpus locked after protocol and search review on [date]. [Names or roles]
reviewed the search strings, provider list, date limits, anchor records,
deduplication output, corpus count, and metadata issues. The snapshot is ready
for title and abstract screening.
```

If the lock is for training, say so:

```text
Corpus locked for training on [date]. This snapshot is for product practice and
must not be used as a completed systematic-review evidence base.
```

## After Locking

Immediately confirm:

- the corpus page shows a locked snapshot;
- the lock reason is visible and understandable;
- screening setup is available;
- assigned reviewers know the protocol criteria;
- any future protocol change will be treated as an amendment.
